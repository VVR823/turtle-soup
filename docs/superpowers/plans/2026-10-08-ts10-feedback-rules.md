## Review Focus

1. Submit races administrator status/permission update: every feedback/source survives, stale read cannot overwrite a newer human decision, audit failure rolls back both.
2. Legacy duplicateID, malformed tail, changed source or an unlinked old report: imports remain repeatable and traceable, no raw player text leaked, no invented reliable turn/cloud permission.
3. A task freezes safe input, then approval is revoked/version changes while a step is waiting: next entry must stop without replacing evidence or pretending already-sent cloud data vanished.
4. Two publish/rollback operations race, or a receipt/body/report/parent is swapped: exactly one CAS wins, no pointer without its audit, old immutable release/cache/session still uses its pinned version.
5. Suspension happens after a turn reservation but before a late answer or evaluation: no stale result charges/writes, restore clearly pauses original game without deleting history or silently changing its rules.
