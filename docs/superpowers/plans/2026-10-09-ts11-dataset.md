## Review Focus

1. A source changes midway through bounded parsing or a physical malformed tail follows valid pairs: no partial freeze, no vanished input, no raw question/error leakage.
2. Family/source/question edges form a transitive chain into an old regression or patch case: the entire candidate group is quarantined, not just one exact duplicate.
3. A human-reviewed case changes only a context, grouping field, label or fact version after review: old review/manifest cannot grant eligibility, even if model_copy bypasses ordinary validation.
4. Two contexts use identical pronouns and opposite valid answers while a context group straddles splits: keep valid distinct contexts in one group, refuse cross-split leakage rather than semantic normalization.
5. A repair loader receives a valid manifest but one blob or role points at private holdout, or write/commit-like output failure occurs: refuse exposure, never overwrite the existing original artifact/report.
